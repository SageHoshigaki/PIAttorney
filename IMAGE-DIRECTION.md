# Image direction

Built-in image generation was used for illustrative imagery; these do not depict actual InventCare staff or facilities.

Shared direction: premium editorial photography, warm limestone and pale oak, muted sage and deep forest green, natural daylight, realistic human expressions, subtle film grain, no logos or visible medical records.

- Hero: wide healthcare pavilion corridor, physician and care coordinator walking on the right, shaded architectural wall on the left for headline space.
- Care: physician listening to a patient in a warm consultation room.
- Diagnostics: calm contemporary MRI suite with warm natural light.
- Partnership: physician, medical coordinator and legal professional in candid working conversation, people on the right and headline space on the left.

Each photograph is used once. Assets are in public/images/.
